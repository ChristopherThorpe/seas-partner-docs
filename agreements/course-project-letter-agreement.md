## 3. Categories of information

**3.1 Partner Information.** "**Partner Information**" means all information, in any form, that Partner or anyone acting on its behalf provides or makes available to Participant in connection with the Project.

**3.2 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means Partner Information that Partner (a) identifies as "Confidential Information," or with a similar legend, in writing at or before the time of disclosure or, if the information is disclosed orally or visually, identifies as confidential at the time of disclosure and confirms in writing within ten (10) business days; or (b) identifies in a notice under Section 4.3. Participant shall not disclose Confidential Information to anyone other than Partner and the other Project Participants. "**Project Participants**" means Participant and each other student or member of the Course's teaching staff who has signed a letter agreement with Partner for the Project on substantially the same terms as this Letter Agreement.

**3.3 Course Private Information.** "**Course Private Information**" means Partner Information that is Course Private Information under the option that applies under Section 4. Participant shall not disclose Course Private Information to anyone other than Partner and members of the Course Community. The "**Course Community**" means (a) students enrolled in the Course during the Course Term; (b) the Course's teaching staff; (c) Harvard faculty and staff who support the administration of the Course; and (d) members of the SEAS community and invited guests attending a Private Course Event, solely while attending that event. A "**Private Course Event**" is a class session, presentation, poster session (including the Course's final poster session), project review or similar event of the Course that is not open to the public and is restricted to members of the SEAS community and invited guests. The "**SEAS community**" means the students, faculty and staff of SEAS.

**3.4 Exclusions.** Information is not Confidential Information or Course Private Information to the extent that Participant can show that it (a) is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Letter Agreement; (b) was known to Participant, without restriction, before Participant received it from Partner; (c) is received by Participant from a third party that is not under a duty of confidentiality regarding it; or (d) is independently developed by Participant without use of Partner Information.

**3.5 Legally required disclosure.** Participant may disclose Confidential Information or Course Private Information to the extent required by law, regulation, subpoena or court order, provided that, where legally permitted, Participant gives Partner prompt written notice and reasonable cooperation so that Partner may seek a protective order or other remedy at Partner's expense.

**3.6 Protected disclosures.** Nothing in this Letter Agreement prohibits Participant from reporting a possible violation of law to a government agency or from making any other disclosure protected by law. Under the Defend Trade Secrets Act, 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), an individual will not be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for the disclosure of a trade secret that is made (a) in confidence to a federal, state or local government official, either directly or indirectly, or to an attorney, solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law; or (b) in a complaint or other document filed in a lawsuit or other proceeding, if that filing is made under seal.

## 5. Course sharing, publication and the Approved Poster

This Section 5 applies whichever option applies under Section 4.

**5.1 Sharing among project teams.** Partner understands that the educational curriculum of the Course involves interactions among project teams and that teams may share Course Private Information within the Course Community. That sharing shall not take place in any public forum without the prior written consent of all applicable Project Participants and Partner.

**5.2 Partner review of other disclosures.** If Participant seeks otherwise to publish findings, in writing or orally, relating to Participant's participation in the Project, Participant shall provide to Partner a copy of any proposed publication, presentation or other disclosure relating in any way to information provided by Partner (or, for an oral disclosure, a written summary of what Participant intends to disclose) (collectively, the "**Proposed Disclosed Info**") at least thirty (30) days before its submission for publication, presentation or other disclosure, and shall not publish, present or otherwise disclose any Proposed Disclosed Info unless and until Partner consents to it in writing or its Publication Hold Period ends. The "**Publication Hold Period**" for any Proposed Disclosed Info begins when Partner receives it and lasts for the period stated in Section 2. If no period is stated there, the Publication Hold Period never ends, and Partner's written consent is always required. The end of a Publication Hold Period does not relieve Participant of Participant's obligations under Sections 3 and 4 regarding Confidential Information and Course Private Information.

**5.3 Private academic disclosures.** Notwithstanding Section 5.2, Participant may make limited disclosures relating to the Project without Partner's prior written consent where the disclosure is made solely in connection with privately submitted coursework, private class presentations, private poster sessions, or other reasonable and related private academic uses associated with the Project, provided that (i) all logos of, and identifying references to, Partner and any Partner Affiliate are masked using generic names (for example, "Climate Technology Firm" or "Investment Bank"); (ii) Participant provides a copy of the coursework, presentation, poster or other disclosure to Partner for its records no later than when it is submitted or presented; and (iii) if Partner identifies concerns with those materials, Partner may request amendments, redactions or similar edits, and Participant shall not unreasonably refuse any such request. "**Partner Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with Partner.

**5.4 Approved Poster.** The "**Approved Poster**" is the final poster that Participant submits for the Project as part of the Course, once Participant has made any amendments, redactions or similar edits that Partner requested under Section 5.3. Notwithstanding Sections 3.3 and 5.2, Participant may orally disclose information in the Approved Poster about the Project to potential employers, graduate programs and similar audiences. To avoid doubt, this Section 5.4 does not permit Participant to publish or otherwise disseminate content in the Approved Poster in written form without Partner's express written consent.

**5.5 Confidential Information.** Nothing in this Section 5 permits Participant to disclose Confidential Information to anyone other than Partner and the other Project Participants.

## 6. Information that Partner does not receive

**6.1 Other projects' information.** Participant shall not disclose to Partner any information of any other project in the Course that, under the terms that apply to that project, either (a) is confidential, including confidential information under a letter agreement between that project's sponsor and its participants; or (b) is restricted to the Course, including course private information under such a letter agreement, and is unrelated to the Project or Partner. Partner shall not request any such information from Participant or any other Project Participant.

**6.2 Student records.** If Participant is a member of the Course's teaching staff, Participant shall not share with Partner any grades or other education records of students, as defined in the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act, 20 U.S.C. § 1232g ("**FERPA Information**"). Partner shall not request FERPA Information. If Partner receives any FERPA Information, Partner shall disregard it and promptly delete or destroy it. This Section 6.2 does not restrict a student from sharing the student's own work with Partner, including copies provided under Section 5.3.
