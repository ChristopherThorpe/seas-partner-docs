## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role | Party? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Harvard** (President and Fellows of Harvard College) | Approves the form of agreement (Exhibit C, signed through OTD, SEAS, or OGC). Holds rights from faculty-sponsored research and third-party agreements. States the conditional informal exception for coursework. | No |
| **SEAS** | The school that offers the Course; part of Harvard, not a separate legal entity. | No |
| **OTD** (Office of Technology Development) | Applies Harvard's IP policy, including the informal exception for coursework. | No |
| **Course Staff** | Everyone who teaches the Course: the instructors and the TAs. | Only if they sign a Project's agreement |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;**Instructors** (course heads) | Assign projects. Approve options other than Open Science, and individual projects. | Only if they sign |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;**TAs** (teaching assistants, teaching fellows, course assistants) | Support and evaluate projects. Contribute no IP. | Only if they sign |
| **Course Community** | Enrolled students, Course Staff, Harvard staff who support the Course, and SEAS community members and invited guests at Private Course Events. The audience for Course Private Information. | No |
| **Partner** (optional; one per Project) | The company sponsoring the Project. | Yes |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;**Authorized officer** (one) | Signs the agreement and any amendment for Partner. | Signs for Partner |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;**Partner Contacts** (one or more) | Give Partner's consents, approvals, and notices. Cannot amend. | Act for Partner |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;**Partner's contributors** (zero or more) | Partner employees who work on the Project; offered credit as co-authors. | No |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;**Partner Affiliates** | Companies under common control with Partner; masked in academic disclosures. | No |
| **Students on the Project** (one or more) | Do the work. One student is an individual project; two or more, a team project. | Yes |
| **TAs on the Project** (zero or more) | Support the team. Sign the Project's agreement only if they need Partner's Confidential Information. | Only if they sign |
| **Student Company** (optional) | A company the students form to use the Project Software under an FSL license. | Bound if it receives an assignment |
| **Other projects in the Course** | The partners, students, and TAs of every other Project. Protected by this Project's agreement (Inadvertent Disclosures, their Course Private Information, reassignment). | No (third-party beneficiaries) |
| **Owners of outside confidential information** | Students' current or former employers, and others whose information a student holds. | No |
| **Licensors of third-party materials** | Owners of open-source and other materials used in the Project. Their licenses pass through. | No |

## E. Intellectual property

| # | Decision | v0.5 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | Three IP options: 8.1 open licenses (including two FSL choices); 8.2 students keep ownership and license Partner and teammates; 8.3 assignment to Partner. | §8.1 to §8.3 |
| E2 | FSL: Partner and the students own the software jointly and license it together. Each may use it for any purpose, including through a Student Company. No one may license it otherwise without an amendment, and no one owes an accounting. | §8.1.1 |
| E3 | Student Company: students founded it, or together hold at least 25% of its voting equity, and no competitor of Partner controls it. | §8.1.1 |
| E4 | Under assignment, publication review applies (D2). Where it can't (Option 4.1), students give 30 days' notice before publishing, and Partner may delay up to 60 days to file a patent. Students keep a license to use the work for demos and portfolios. | §8.3, §8.7 |
| E5 | Partner keeps its background IP. Improvements and derived works are Project Work Product. Students may use Partner's materials only for the Project during the Course. | §8.5 |
| E6 | Third-party materials keep their own licenses and are not assigned or licensed. | §8.6 |
| E7 | Course Staff contribute no IP. Their evaluations and grades are not Project Work Product. | §8.4 |
| E8 | Work is accepted as-is. Students give no warranties. Partner releases students from its own claims, but doesn't defend or indemnify them. | §7.1 to §7.4 |
| E9 | A student reassigned from another project is bound by that project's terms for work already done there, and keeps its partner's information confidential. | §11.11 |
| E10 | Harvard's rights from faculty-sponsored research or third-party agreements come first. Otherwise Harvard claims no rights in coursework under its conditional informal exception. | §9.2, Exhibit A.DS §2, Exhibit C |
