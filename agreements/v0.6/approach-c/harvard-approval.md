**Acknowledgments.** In giving this approval, Harvard acknowledges that:

- Harvard is not a party to any Course Project Master Agreement or Project Schedule, and has no obligations under them (Master Agreement Section 9.1);
- as a conditional informal exception to its Statement of Policy in Regard to Intellectual Property, Harvard claims no rights in intellectual property that students create in their coursework, if the only Harvard-administered resources they use are those generally provided to students in the course (Master Agreement Section 9.3);
- that exception is subject to Harvard's rights from faculty-sponsored research, from agreements between Harvard and third parties, and under its policies (Master Agreement Section 9.2);
- members of the course staff sign in a personal capacity and do not bind Harvard (Master Agreement Section 4.1); and
- Harvard may enforce Sections 9 and 10.5 of each Master Agreement, which protect it, as a third-party beneficiary.
