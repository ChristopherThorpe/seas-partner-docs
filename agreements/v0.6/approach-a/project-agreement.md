Each Signer is responsible only for their own acts, and no one answers for a Teammate.

**1.2 Course Agreement.** This agreement uses the Course Agreement for the Course, version [0.6], which Partner has received. Terms defined there have the same meanings here, except that in this agreement "Signer" means a Signer of this agreement, and "Partner" means the Partner named on the cover page unless the text refers to another project's Partner. In a Course Agreement section, both have their Course Agreement meanings. Partner agrees, as if it had signed the Course Agreement, to these Course Agreement sections:

- 7.2 (Harvard's rights come first);

### 2. Partner information

**2.1 Partner Information.** "**Partner Information**" is all information, in any form, that Partner or anyone acting for it gives or makes available to a Signer for the Project, including before the Effective Date.

**2.2 The information choice.** The choice on the cover page applies. Unless the Open, Course Private, and Confidential choice applies, Partner must make all commercially reasonable efforts not to give information it considers confidential.

(a) Open. Partner must not give any restricted information, and no Partner Information is Course Private Information or Confidential Information. If Partner gives restricted information anyway and claims it is confidential, the Signers handle it as an Inadvertent Disclosure under Course Agreement Section 3.1: they tell Partner, do not use it, and delete it.

(b) Open and Course Private. All Partner Information is considered Course Private Information unless marked Open. Signers may share Course Private Information within the Course Community, or, subject to Section 2.3(c), as Partner approves in writing, and nowhere else.

(c) Open, Course Private, and Confidential. Paragraph (b) applies, and Partner may also mark Partner Information as Confidential Information: in writing when Partner gives it or, for information given orally or visually, by written confirmation within ten business days. Partner and the Signers may give Confidential Information only to Partner and the Signers, or as Partner approves in writing. In particular, they must not give it to any member of the Course Staff who has not signed this agreement.

(d) Mistakes. If Partner gives restricted information by mistake and then identifies it in writing as confidential, each Signer must promptly stop using it and delete every copy. If coursework already submitted contains it, the Signer must make reasonable efforts to have it removed and resubmit the coursework without it. Uses before Partner's notice that were permitted for Course Private Information are not a breach.

**2.3 Protecting restricted information.** Each Signer must (a) use Course Private Information and Confidential Information only for the Project and the Course, and as Sections 3 and 4 permit; (b) protect it with at least Reasonable Care; (c) keep it out of public forums, including public websites, public code repositories, social media, and public events, unless, for Course Private Information, Partner and all of the student Signers consent in writing; and (d) follow Course Agreement Section 3.4 on generative AI tools.

**2.4 Partner's duties.** Partner must not give any Signer information, software, or technology listed on the Commerce Control List of the U.S. Export Administration Regulations or the U.S. Munitions List of the International Traffic in Arms Regulations. Partner must label personal data, third-party data, and other data whose use or disclosure is restricted by law or contract as Course Private Information or Confidential Information, and must give no such data under the Open choice. Partner must not ask any Signer for students' grades or other education records, or for another project's Course Private Information or Confidential Information unless that project's students and Partner (if any) consent in writing. If Partner receives any of these, it must handle them as an Inadvertent Disclosure.

**2.5 End of the Project.** Before a student Signer's End Date, and before Course computing resources holding Project Work Product are shut down, the student must make reasonable efforts to ensure that Partner receives copies of the Project Work Product it is entitled to. Within 30 days after a Signer's End Date, or sooner if Partner asks in writing, the Signer must (a) delete all Confidential Information, including from personal devices, cloud accounts, and generative AI tool histories, except data and code licensed under Section 3.6; (b) share any Course Private Information they retain only as Section 2.2(b) allows; (c) return or delete any access credentials from Partner, and stop using Partner's systems, accounts, and keys; and (d) if Partner asks, confirm in writing that these steps are done. A Partner Contact may let a Signer keep more, by email.

For example, a student may demonstrate a model trained on Partner data, but may not reuse, distill, reverse engineer, extract, or disclose that data. Licensed data and code stay subject to Section 2 while the Enabling License lasts. The Enabling License does not permit commercial use, and no commercial use of Project Work Product may use anything licensed under it.
