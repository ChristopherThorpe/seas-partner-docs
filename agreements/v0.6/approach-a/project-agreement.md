## Standard terms

### 1. The Project

**1.1 Parties.** Partner and each individual who signs the cover page or a joinder page (a "**signer**") are parties. Partner and each student signer agree with each other, and each student signer also agrees with every other student signer. A member of the Course Staff signs only to receive Confidential Information, agrees with Partner, and is bound only by Sections 1, 2, and 6. Each signer is responsible only for their own acts, and no one answers for a teammate.

**1.2 Course Agreement.** This agreement uses the Course Agreement for the Course, version [0.6], which Partner has received. Terms defined there have the same meanings here, except that in this agreement "signer" means a signer of this agreement, while in a Course Agreement section it means a signer of the Course Agreement. Partner agrees to Course Agreement Sections 3.1 (Inadvertent Disclosures), 3.2 (Other projects' Course Private Information, as it applies to information Partner receives through the Course), 7.2 (Harvard's rights come first), 7.4 (no liability of Harvard or the Course Staff), and 8.2 (exclusions and permitted disclosures) as if it had signed the Course Agreement. Harvard may enforce Sections 7.2 and 7.4, and the Course Staff Section 7.4, against Partner. Each student signer agrees with Partner and every other signer to Course Agreement Sections 2.3, 3, 5, and 8, whether or not they have signed it. This agreement adds to the Course Agreement and does not change it. If the two conflict, the Course Agreement governs.

**1.3 Coursework.** Partner sponsors the Project as part of an academic course. The Project is coursework: the Course Staff alone set its academic requirements, supervise it, and evaluate and grade the students. Partner may benefit from the results.

**1.4 No employment.** Partner does not hire, pay, or engage any signer, Harvard, or anyone else in the Course under this agreement. No employment, agency, partnership, joint-venture, or fiduciary relationship arises from it, and no signer may act for Partner. Partner need not offer, and no signer need accept, a job or other engagement.

**1.5 Signing and joining.** There is one Project Agreement for the Project, and its choices apply to every student signer. Students and Course Staff sign it when Course Agreement Sections 2.2 and 4.3 require. A person who joins later signs a joinder page and is bound as of the Effective Date, and joining is not a change to this agreement. A signer who leaves the Project remains bound for the work done and information received before their End Date.

### 2. Partner information

**2.1 Partner Information.** "**Partner Information**" is all information, in any form, that Partner or anyone acting for it gives or makes available to a signer for the Project, including before the Effective Date.

**2.2 The information choice.** The choice on the cover page applies.

(a) *Open.* Partner must not give any restricted information, and no Partner Information is Course Private Information or Confidential Information. If Partner gives information anyway and says it is confidential, the signers handle it as an Inadvertent Disclosure under Course Agreement Section 3.1: they tell Partner, do not use it, and delete it.

(b) *Course Private.* All Partner Information is Course Private Information. Signers may share it within the Course Community, or as Partner approves in writing, and nowhere else. Unless the Course Private and Confidential choice applies, Partner must use reasonable efforts not to give information it considers confidential.

(c) *Course Private and Confidential.* Paragraph (b) applies, and Partner may also mark Partner Information as Confidential Information: in writing when Partner gives it or, for information given orally or visually, by written confirmation within ten business days. Partner and the signers may give Confidential Information only to Partner and the signers, or as Partner approves in writing. In particular, they must not give it to any member of the Course Staff who has not signed this agreement.

(d) *Mistakes.* Under either Course Private choice, if Partner gives information by mistake and then identifies it in writing as confidential, each signer must promptly stop using it and delete every copy. If coursework already submitted contains it, the signer must make reasonable efforts to have it removed and resubmit the coursework without it. Uses before Partner's notice that were permitted for Course Private Information are not a breach.

**2.3 Protecting restricted information.** Each signer must (a) use Course Private Information and Confidential Information only for the Project and the Course, and as Sections 3 and 4 permit; (b) protect it with at least reasonable care; (c) keep it out of public forums, including public websites, public code repositories, social media, and public events, unless, for Course Private Information, Partner and all of the student signers consent in writing; and (d) follow Course Agreement Section 3.4 on generative AI tools.

**2.4 Partner's duties.** Partner must not give any signer information, software, or technology listed on the Commerce Control List of the U.S. Export Administration Regulations or the U.S. Munitions List of the International Traffic in Arms Regulations. Partner must label personal data, third-party data, and other data whose use or disclosure is restricted by law or contract as Course Private Information or Confidential Information, and must give no such data under the Open choice. Partner must not ask any signer for students' grades or other education records, or for another project's Course Private Information or Confidential Information unless that project's partner and students consent in writing. If Partner receives any of these, it must handle them as an Inadvertent Disclosure.

**2.5 End of the Project.** Before a student signer's End Date, and before Course computing resources holding Project Work Product are shut down, the student must make reasonable efforts to ensure that Partner receives copies of the Project Work Product it is entitled to. Within 30 days after a signer's End Date, or sooner if Partner asks in writing, the signer must (a) delete all Confidential Information, including from personal devices, cloud accounts, and generative AI tool histories, except data and code licensed under Section 3.6; (b) keep any Course Private Information they retain within the Course Community; (c) return or delete any access credentials from Partner, and stop using Partner's systems, accounts, and keys; and (d) if Partner asks, confirm in writing that these steps are done. A Partner Contact may let a signer keep more, by email.

Under the Open choice, however, the student may publish and distribute them, with Partner still masked under Section 3.1(a) unless Partner agrees otherwise in writing.

**3.3 Publication review.** This Section 3.3 applies if Partner requests review on the cover page, or if Partner owns the Project Work Product, unless the information choice is Open. It covers any publication, presentation, or other disclosure of findings that relate in any way to Partner Information and, if Partner owns the Project Work Product, of any of that work. A student signer must give Partner a copy of the proposed disclosure, or for an oral disclosure a written summary, at least 30 days before submitting it. The student must not make the disclosure until Partner consents in writing or the "**Publication Hold Period**" ends. That period starts when Partner receives the copy and lasts for the time stated on the cover page or, if none is stated, until Partner consents. Partner must not unreasonably withhold consent to publishing results that contain no Confidential Information. The end of the hold does not end any duty under Section 2.

**3.4 Credit.**

(a) Before Partner submits material about the Project Work Product for publication, it must offer each student signer co-authorship, unless the material covers substantial work done after the Duration. Co-authors must get reasonable time to review the draft and request changes, and other student signers reasonable time to review it and comment. A student signer who is not a co-author must be credited in the acknowledgments, unless they ask not to be named.

(b) A student signer who submits such material must offer Partner's employees who contributed to the Project the same credit and review that Course Agreement Section 5.6 requires for teammates.

(c) Anyone may be asked to sign a reasonable nondisclosure agreement before receiving a confidential draft. This Section 3.4 does not apply to the uses in Sections 3.1 and 3.2, and does not displace Section 3.3. Course Agreement Section 5.6(c) says how this process may be changed.

**3.5 Demonstrations.** A student signer may demonstrate working Project Work Product live to potential employers, graduate programs, and similar audiences, even if it shows Course Private Information. The student must mask Partner as Section 3.1(a) requires, disclose no Confidential Information, and give no copies of the Project Work Product or of data or code licensed under Section 3.6. A student signer may also record video demonstrations, with Confidential Information removed, and share them non-publicly (for example, on a password-protected website). That sharing needs Partner's consent, which Partner must not unreasonably withhold. Section 3.3 does not apply to these demonstrations.

**3.6 Enabling License.** Partner grants each student signer a license, for the Confidentiality Period, to use limited or simulated versions of data or code that Partner provided (the "**Enabling License**"). It allows only what is minimally needed for noncommercial demonstrations, including under Section 3.5 and in a portfolio. It covers the data and code that a Partner Contact identifies by written notice. Partner must use commercially reasonable efforts to identify enough for those demonstrations, and the Partner Contacts decide in their discretion what to identify. For example, a student may demonstrate a model trained on Partner data, but may not reuse, distill, reverse engineer, extract, or disclose that data. Licensed data and code stay subject to Section 2 while the Enabling License lasts. A student may demonstrate what licensed software does, but must not disclose its source code without Partner's written consent. The Enabling License does not permit commercial use, and no commercial use of Project Work Product may use anything licensed under it. Job interviews, research discussions, and similar activities are not commercial use.

Partner grants no other rights in it, except under Section 3.6. Materials that others own keep their own licenses (Course Agreement Section 1.2).

**4.2 The choices.** The ownership and license choices on the cover page apply to all Project Work Product of every student signer. "**Project Software**" is the software in the Project Work Product, in source and object form, and "**Other Work Product**" is all other Project Work Product. (c) Each student signer licenses their rights in the Project Software to everyone who receives a copy. The license is the software license chosen on the cover page: the MIT License, the Apache License 2.0, both (each recipient may choose either), or the other license named there.

(d) Each student signer licenses their rights in Other Work Product, to everyone who receives a copy, under the license chosen on the cover page: CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 4.0, or the other license named there.

### 6. General

**6.1 Authorized officer and Partner Contacts.** Partner's authorized officer signs this agreement, any change to it, and any waiver by Partner. The Partner Contacts may act for Partner on the Project, but may not change this agreement. They may give consents and approvals, give and identify Partner Information, request edits, give notices, and receive copies and requests. Partner may replace or add Partner Contacts by notice to the signers, and that is not a change to this agreement.

**6.2 Notices.** Notices, consents, copies, and requests must be in writing, and email is enough. Copies and requests for consent go to the Partner Contacts. Other notices to Partner go to its authorized officer and the Partner Contacts. Notices to a signer go to the email address in their signature entry, or to another address they give by notice.

**6.3 Changes.** The choices and blanks on the cover page, other than the Partner Contacts, may be changed only in a writing signed by Partner's authorized officer and every student signer. A student signer who stopped taking part in the Project before the end of the Duration need not sign. If the result is not Open Science, the Course instructor must approve it in writing or by email, and Partner certifies that approval in the change. A change binds only those who sign it, and cannot take back rights that others already received, such as a public license to a copy already released. A change to the information choice applies only to Partner Information given after the change, unless the change says otherwise. Before the end of the Duration, nothing else in this agreement may be changed, apart from the consents, approvals, notices, and joinders it allows. After the Duration, it may be changed only in a writing signed by Partner and every signer. A waiver must be in writing, and a delay in enforcing a term is not a waiver.

**6.4 Assignment.** Partner may assign this agreement, on notice to the signers, to a successor to all or substantially all of the business to which it relates, and the Partner License goes with it. Signers may not assign this agreement, except as Section 4.4(f) allows. This agreement binds and benefits the parties' permitted successors and assigns.

**6.5 Injunctions.** A breach of Section 2, 3, or 4 may cause irreparable harm, and Partner or any student signer may seek an injunction or other equitable relief in addition to any other remedy.

**6.6 Harvard.** Harvard is not a party to this agreement and has no obligations under it. Partner confirms that the copy of the Course Agreement it received had its course details completed, including the boxes under "Harvard's rights in students' work".

**6.7 Authority and acknowledgment.** Each party confirms that it may enter into and perform this agreement, that each individual signer has reached the age of majority, and that the individual signing for Partner is authorized to do so. Partner certifies the Course instructor's approvals as the cover page and Section 6.3 require. Each student signer confirms that they have read this agreement, have had the chance to ask questions and consult advisors, sign voluntarily, and could instead have asked for an Open Science project or a project without a partner.

Signatures may be electronic, including through Adobe Acrobat Sign, and counterparts together form one agreement. An unenforceable term is enforced as far as the law allows, and the rest stays in effect.

**6.9 Survival.** This agreement continues after the Duration and after each signer's End Date, except the license in Section 4.1. Sections 3.1 to 3.3 continue without time limit.
